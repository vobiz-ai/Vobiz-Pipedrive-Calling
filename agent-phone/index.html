    Frequently asked

    Why does the person I call hear ringing after answering?

    Vobiz dials the customer first, then bridges your browser in. Those few seconds of ringback are that second leg connecting.

    Why is the Call button disabled?

    Calling needs both a signed-in account and a live SIP registration. The status badge indicates connection state.

    Where do call recordings go?

    Calls are recorded on Vobiz, displayed in the list above, and automatically logged to matching contacts and deals in Pipedrive.
